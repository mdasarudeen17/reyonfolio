03/ About me

Learning people finish and remember.

I design learning that people finish and remember. For more than four years I have turned dense subject matter into microlearning, self-paced courses and short explainer videos that respect a learner’s time.

I start from the performance gap and work backwards, using adult learning principles, Gagné’s events of instruction, Bloom’s taxonomy and Kirkpatrick’s levels to check that it worked.

I build in Storyline 360, Rise 360 and Vyond, and publish to any LMS through SCORM.
